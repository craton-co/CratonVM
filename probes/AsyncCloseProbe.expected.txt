One `want` was corrected against the measurement rather than the other way
round: `channelWrite` answers `ClosedChannelException` on HotSpot 25.0.3,
because the parked write returns its partial count and the NEXT iteration of
the row's write loop finds the channel already closed. All three of
AsynchronousCloseException / ClosedChannelException / a partial count are
wakeups; only TIMEOUT is the defect this family is about.
