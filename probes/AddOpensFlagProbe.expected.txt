Both arms below are byte-identical between HotSpot and CratonVM after the
`ALL-UNNAMED` fix in vm/src/config.rs + classloading/src/module.rs. Before it,
CratonVM's WITH-flag arm differed on exactly one line:

    open java.net unqualified=true      <-- CratonVM, pre-fix (wrong)
    open java.net unqualified=false     <-- HotSpot, and CratonVM post-fix

because the launcher folded the `ALL-UNNAMED` target into the empty string,
which `ModuleRegistry` reads as an *unqualified* open — granting every module
in the process, not just the unnamed one, and making `Module.isOpen("java.net")`
answer true.

Reading the two arms:

* `URL.factory setAccessible` flipping InaccessibleObjectException -> OK is the
  whole of what Spring Boot's `DirtiesUrlFactoriesExtension` needs, and it is
  what makes the flag load-bearing rather than decorative. A probe green in
  both arms would prove nothing.
* `open java.util toSelf` and `ArrayList.elementData setAccessible` are the
  controls: `--add-opens=java.base/java.net` must not move either, in either
  VM. They are what tells a working grant apart from a gate that stopped
  enforcing.
* `exported java.net toSelf=true` in both arms is not a bug: java.base
  *exports* java.net unconditionally. Export is shallow access; the open is
  what deep reflection needs, and only the open moves.
